How much do ceramics classes in Barcelona cost?
At La Mesa the 2-hour class costs €45 (hand-building) or €65 (wheel). The hand-building and wheel courses cost €120/month for 4 two-hour classes. Hour packs: 8 h · €120, 16 h · €220, 24 h · €300. Materials and firings included.
Are materials included?
Yes. All our classes include tools, materials and firings.
Can I book online?
Yes. The 2-hour class, the courses and the hour packs are booked directly online.
Where is La Mesa?
We are at Carrer de l'Atlàntida 47, in the La Barceloneta neighbourhood, Barcelona. A 5-minute walk from the beach.
Can I try pottery with no previous experience?
Yes. The 2-hour class needs no previous experience, and the courses are designed for complete beginners: we start from zero and you go at your own pace.
Do you host private events, team buildings or hen parties?
Yes, we organise private sessions for groups of up to 10 people: birthdays, hen parties, team buildings and more. Message us on WhatsApp or by email for a quote.
When can I pick up my piece?
The whole process takes about three to four weeks. We’ll let you know on WhatsApp when your pieces are ready to collect.

Do you make tableware for restaurants?
Yes. Plates, small plates, bowls and cups in stoneware, made by hand in our studio, to buy or to rent, in the sizes and colours your kitchen needs. There’s no minimum order and we ship across Europe; rental is only available in the Barcelona area. We give you a specific date in the quote.
