Ceramics Classes in Barcelona: La Mesa Format Guide

By the La Mesa team · Ceramics studio in La Barceloneta

When choosing ceramics classes in Barcelona, consider both the technique and how often you want to attend. At La Mesa in Barceloneta, the options are two-hour experiences, weekly classes and hour packs. This guide explains our formats so you can match a first visit or an ongoing practice to your schedule.

Hand-building experience: try a single session

The hand-building experience is a two-hour class working with clay at the table. No previous experience is needed: the team helps you choose a manageable form and shows you how to build it.

It suits a first introduction, a creative outing or a chance to try the studio before committing to regular classes. Your project is adapted to the available time, so you do not need to arrive with a finished design. Check the experience page for dates and spaces.

Wheel experience: explore a different technique

The wheel class introduces shaping clay on a rotating wheel. During the two-hour session you practise centring, opening and pulling the walls, with a maximum of two participants.

Beginners can start here too. The small format allows guidance through those first movements, while building control takes practice. Read our wheel-throwing guide if you want to compare the process with hand-building.

Weekly classes: follow projects through

Weekly classes include four two-hour sessions per month. Choose between weekly hand-building and weekly wheel throwing according to the technique you want to develop.

Returning regularly gives you time to revisit a shape, practise a particular difficulty and see your work at different stages. This format suits a recurring space in your calendar. Check the available group and timetable before planning your routine.

Hour packs: spread your sessions out

Packs of 8, 16 or 24 hours offer another way to organise several visits. They may suit you if you want to continue but cannot attend on a fixed day each week. Ask about pack conditions, compatible sessions and availability before choosing.

Our La Mesa pricing guide puts experience, weekly class and hour-pack prices together so you can compare the costs in one place.

What the formats include

Class materials and firings are included: clay, tools and glazes are provided. Pieces stay at the studio to dry and go through firing and finishing. Collection is arranged once they are ready. If you are visiting Barcelona, discuss your departure date before booking.

We can teach in English, Spanish and Portuguese; confirm the language for your chosen session. The studio is at Carrer de l'Atlàntida, 47, Barceloneta, Barcelona.

Which option fits your plans?

For a first visit, choose the hand-building or wheel experience according to what you want to try. For a regular practice, check the weekly timetable. To spread several visits across your calendar, ask about the hour packs.
