Personalised ceramic gifts in Barcelona, handmade for someone special

By the La Mesa team · Ceramics studio in La Barceloneta

A mug with an inside joke. A bowl inspired by a favourite hobby. A plate marking a date worth remembering. At La Mesa, we make personalised ceramic gifts by hand in La Barceloneta, Barcelona, with prices starting from €40. Your piece takes 2–3 weeks to make once we have agreed on the design. You can collect it from the studio or arrange shipping.

What can you personalise?

Choose names, initials, dates, messages or drawings, as well as colours, sizes and custom shapes. An idea, a reference photo or a rough sketch is enough to get started. You do not need drawing skills or a finished design: we will help you decide on the piece and its details.

Before we start, we agree on the shape, finish and placement of each detail. A phrase might follow the rim of a bowl, a drawing might sit on the outside, or a message might be tucked inside. The aim is to make something that feels right for the person receiving it.

Ceramic gift ideas for someone special

  • A mug with their name or an inside joke: a little reminder of you to go with their morning coffee.
  • A plate with a special date: a way to mark an anniversary, a wedding or a moment you want to remember.
  • A bowl in their favourite colours: a gift for a new home or someone who loves setting the table.
  • A piece with a meaningful drawing: a favourite hobby, a family memory or a motif they will recognise straight away.

We adapt each idea to the piece and your budget. For inspiration, explore the shapes and finishes in La Mesa’s bespoke ceramics.

A closer look at personalised ceramic gifts

How much does a personalised ceramic gift cost?

You can commission a personalised ceramic gift from €40. The final price depends on the type of piece, its size and the complexity of the design. Treat €40 as a starting point; we will prepare a quote for the gift you have in mind.

Tell us your idea and how much you would like to spend. We will confirm the design and price before we start. If you need shipping, we will also confirm the cost and arrangements.

How far in advance should you order?

Your piece takes 2–3 weeks to make once the final design is agreed. Allow extra time beforehand to discuss your idea and settle the details. The making process includes shaping, drying, glazing and firing.

If you are ordering for a birthday, wedding or Christmas, mention the date in your first message. We can then confirm whether the timing works before we start. Shipping adds time to the production period.

How to order your gift, step by step

  1. Tell us your idea on WhatsApp. Let us know who it is for, the occasion, your budget and when you need it.
  2. Share the details. Send the name, message or drawing, along with any colour or shape references.
  3. Confirm the design and quote. We will go through the details with you before making your piece.
  4. Collect your piece or arrange shipping. We will help you arrange collection or shipping.

Studio pickup in Barcelona or shipping

Collect your piece from La Mesa, Carrer de l’Atlàntida, 47, La Barceloneta, Barcelona. Shipping is also available. Tell us where the gift needs to go so we can confirm the cost, delivery arrangements and estimated transit time. Shipping time is additional to the 2–3 weeks needed to make your piece.

Handmade pieces can vary slightly in shape and finish. Before making yours, we will check the details that matter: names and spelling, the date, the drawing and how you plan to use the piece.

Frequently asked questions about personalised gifts

Can I order just one piece?

Yes, you can order a single piece. Personalised gifts start from €40, with the final price confirmed once we know the design.

Do I need a finished design?

No. You can contact us with an idea, a sketch or a reference image. We agree on the design together before production begins.

Can you add names, messages or drawings?

Yes. We can add names, dates, messages and drawings, and agree on the colours, size and shape of your piece with you.

How long will it take?

Production takes 2–3 weeks after the design is agreed. If you need shipping, allow time for transport too. Tell us when you need your gift so we can confirm whether the timing works before we start.
