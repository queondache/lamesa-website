Creative Workshops in Barcelona: Which One to Choose

By the La Mesa team · Ceramics studio in La Barceloneta

If you are looking for a creative workshop in Barcelona where you actually make something with your hands, pottery is one of the most rewarding options: no experience is needed and you end up with a piece of your own. At La Mesa in Barceloneta you can book a 2-hour hand-building class (€45), a private wheel class for one or two people (2 hours, €65), a weekly course (€120 a month), an hour pack or a private session for your group. Workshops with guest artists are priced individually.

This guide helps you pick the right format depending on how much time you have, who you are coming with and what you want to learn.

What kinds of creative workshops are there?

Searches for workshops in Barcelona tend to lump very different things together. Before booking, it helps to know which one you are after:

  • One-off class: try a technique in a couple of hours, with no commitment.
  • Ongoing course: come back every week and build real skill.
  • Themed workshop: a session around one topic or technique, often led by a guest.
  • Private session: the workshop is run just for your group, birthday or team.

Whatever the format, check how long it lasts, whether materials are included, how many people will be there and what you take home.

Hand-building: the easiest way in

In the hand-building class you shape clay at the table, no machines involved: pinching, coils and slabs. It is the most relaxed format for a first time, and you can chat while everyone builds their own piece, such as a bowl, a cup or a small plate. It lasts two hours and costs €45.

The pottery wheel: if the wheel is calling you

The wheel class is private, for one or two people, and costs €65. You learn to centre the clay and raise a first shape with guidance. Come ready to practise: the wheel takes patience, and one session is about understanding it, not mastering it. For more background, read our beginner’s guide to wheel throwing.

Workshops with guest artists

Beyond pottery classes, La Mesa is a creative lab that hosts workshops with guest artists. Each one has its own theme, length and price, which varies. When a workshop is confirmed, dates, price and places are published on the workshop page; if nothing is listed, ask the studio about the current programme.

Want to keep going? Courses and hour packs

If one class leaves you wanting more, the weekly course gives you four 2-hour sessions a month for €120, in hand-building or on the wheel. You request your first class online from the course page.

If you would rather come when you can, the hour pack lets you practise hand-building or wheel at your own pace: 8 hours for €120, 16 hours for €220 or 24 hours for €300.

Groups, birthdays and team building

For a celebration, a hen party or a company activity, a private session is run just for you: up to 10 people at the Barceloneta studio, or up to 40 if we bring the workshop to your office or another venue. It usually lasts two to three hours and is quoted to measure. See our pottery team building guide for more.

How to book and what to know before you come

  • Hand-building or wheel class: pick a date in the calendar on the class page and pay online. If your time is not shown, message us on WhatsApp.
  • Weekly course: request your first class online from the course page.
  • Hour pack: bought online.
  • Workshops and private sessions: via WhatsApp.

We are open Tuesday to Saturday at Carrer de l'Atlàntida, 47. Classes are taught in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and are open to anyone from 8 years old. Single classes can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before. Wear clothes you do not mind getting dirty.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience to join a pottery workshop?

No. La Mesa’s hand-building and wheel classes also welcome people who have never touched clay, with guidance throughout the session.

How much does a creative pottery workshop at La Mesa cost?

A 2-hour hand-building class costs €45, a private wheel class (up to 2 people) €65 and the weekly course €120 a month. Guest-artist workshops are priced individually and private sessions are quoted to measure.

Can I take my piece home the same day?

No. The piece needs to dry, be glazed and be fired. After a single class it is ready to collect about 15 days later; if you are leaving Barcelona sooner, ask about shipping on WhatsApp.

Can we come as a group?

Yes. Private sessions host up to 10 people at the Barceloneta studio, and for groups of up to 40 we bring the workshop to your office or another venue. Each session is quoted to measure.
