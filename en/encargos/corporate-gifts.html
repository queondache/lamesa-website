A gift people use every day

We are Lau and Vick, two ceramicists with a studio in La Barceloneta. We make every piece by hand in high-fire stoneware. A ceramic mug or bowl does not end up in a drawer: it is used for the morning coffee and reminds people who gave it.

The pieces people give most

We start from our shapes and adapt them to your gift. If you need another size or shape, we make it.

Mugs and cups

The most practical gift: coffee, tea or a small object on the desk.

Small plates and bowls

For keys, jewellery, snacks or dessert. They work well as a series, one per person.

Your name, your date, your message

We can add names, initials, dates, messages and drawings, and choose the glaze colour with you. A series can be identical for everyone or carry each person's name.

An idea, a reference photo or a sketch is enough. We review the design together before we start. Each piece is handmade, so there are small differences in tone and shape from one to the next.

How we work

  1. Contact

    Tell us the occasion, the pieces, the approximate quantity and the date.

  2. Proposal

    We send you shapes, colours and a personalisation idea. If you like, come to the studio to see them.

  3. Quote

    Fixed, with pieces, design, price and delivery date.

  4. Production and delivery

    We make the pieces by hand and you collect them at the studio or we ship them.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a ceramic corporate gift cost?

From €40 per personalised piece. The final price depends on the piece, its size and the design, and goes down with volume on pieces of the same type. We send you a fixed quote.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We make anything from a single piece to a series for the whole team.

How long does it take?

Production takes 2–3 weeks once the design is agreed. For large series we give you a specific date in the quote. Shipping time is extra.

What can be personalised?

Names, initials, dates, messages and drawings, plus the glaze colour. We agree the design with you before we start.

Do you ship to other offices?

Yes, we ship across Europe. You can also collect from our studio in La Barceloneta.

Are they dishwasher and microwave safe?

Yes, both. Not for the oven. They are food safe.
