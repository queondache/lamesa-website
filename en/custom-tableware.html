Your sizes, your colours

Any piece can be made in your own sizes and any shape in your own colours. We start from your menu and your dining room: you tell us about the dish, the pass and the space, and we suggest shapes, sizes and glazes.

It is a dedicated project, with its own schedule and its own quote. If you want to see and handle the pieces, visit the studio by appointment.

Buy or rent

You get a fixed price in the quote, based on shapes, quantities and colours.

Buy

The pieces are yours, in the quantity you need.

  • No minimum order: from a single piece.
  • Volume discounts on pieces of the same type.
  • We ship across Europe.

Rent

Designed for seasonal menus: change the tableware when you change the menu.

  • Periods of 4, 6 or 12 months. The longer the period, the lower the price per piece.
  • Deposit of one month's fee, returned at the end.
  • Breakages included up to 5% of the pieces.
  • Barcelona area only, with delivery and collection included.

How we work

  1. Contact

    You write to us with the type of cuisine, the pieces and approximate quantities.

  2. Visit or proposal

    You visit the studio to see shapes and glazes, or we send you photos and a proposal.

  4. Production

    We make the pieces by hand. Lead time is a few weeks, depending on quantity and colours.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order?

No. We make anything from a single piece for your home to full series for a restaurant.

How long does it take?

A few weeks, depending on quantity, shapes and colours. The quote includes a specific date.

Are they dishwasher and microwave safe?

Yes, both. Not oven safe. They are safe for food contact.

Can you make a size or colour that is not in the catalogue?

Yes. The catalogue sizes are those of our pieces; other sizes and colours are made to order and quoted separately.

Are all pieces identical?

Each piece is made by hand, so there are small differences in tone and shape from one to the next. Within a series we keep sizes and glaze consistent.

How does renting work?

You rent the pieces for 4, 6 or 12 months, with a deposit of one month's fee that is returned at the end. It is available in the Barcelona area only, and delivery and collection are included.

What if a rented piece breaks?

Breakages are included up to 5% of the pieces. Beyond that, each broken piece is charged at the purchase price.

Do you ship outside Barcelona?

Yes, we ship across Europe. Renting, however, is only available in the Barcelona area.

Can I visit the studio?

Yes, by appointment. We are at Carrer de l'Atlàntida, 47, in Barceloneta.
