In short

La Mesa’s hand-building class is a 2-hour pottery class in La Barceloneta, Barcelona, where you discover clay by shaping it with your hands, no wheel involved. It costs €45 per person and no experience is needed: it’s designed for complete beginners, with guidance. You learn hand-building techniques, finishes and glazes. Clay, tools, glazes and firing of your piece are all included; it’s ready about 15 days later, and if you leave Barcelona before then we can ship it to your home (ask for the shipping price on WhatsApp). Classes are taught in English, Spanish or Portuguese, from 8 years old, Tuesday to Saturday at the time you prefer within studio hours, subject to availability. The studio is at Carrer de l’Atlàntida, 47, close to the beach and to Barceloneta metro (L4). Pick a day and time in the calendar on this page or message us on WhatsApp.

What happens in the 2 hours

You don’t need to bring anything: La Mesa provides the clay, tools and materials. For two hours you work the clay with your hands and, with guidance the whole time, learn hand-building techniques, finishes and glazes. You don’t need to have touched clay before.

When you finish, your piece stays at the studio: glazes and firing are already included in the price. About 15 days later it’s ready to collect, or we can ship it home if you’ve already left Barcelona.
