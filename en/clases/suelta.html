What you will do

  1. We welcome you at our table and you choose: hand-building or the wheel.
  2. For 2 hours we guide you step by step to shape your piece, at your own pace.
  3. You choose the colours: glazes are included.
  4. We fire it in our kiln. You pick it up after 15 days, or we ship it to you.

Who welcomes you

La Mesa is a creative studio in La Barceloneta run by Lau and Vick. We are not a school: it is a small space where you come to try, create and enjoy the process.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience?

No. The class is designed for beginners: we guide you step by step from the first touch of the clay.

Is the class in English?

Yes. We teach in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Can I take my piece home?

Your piece is ready 15 days after the class, once it has dried and been fired. If you are leaving Barcelona sooner, we can ship it to your home: ask us for the price to your country.

What is the difference between hand-building and the wheel?

In hand-building (€45) you shape the clay with your hands. The wheel (€65) is a private class for up to 2 people: you learn to centre, open and pull up the clay.

What does the price include?

Clay, tools, glazes and the firing of your piece. The class lasts 2 hours.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before the class.

Can children come?

Yes, from 8 years old.

Is the studio accessible?

Yes, the studio is wheelchair accessible.

Where is La Mesa?

At Carrer de l'Atlàntida 47, in La Barceloneta (Barcelona), a short walk from the beach and from Barceloneta metro station (L4).

Can I come at a time that isn't in the calendar?

Yes, Tuesday to Saturday within our opening hours. Message us on WhatsApp and we will find you a time.

Can we come as a group?

Yes. For birthdays, hen parties, team building or groups of friends, see groups & events.

What if I want to come every week?

There is the weekly class: 4 sessions of 2 hours, always the same day and time. Hand-building €120/month and wheel throwing €120/month.
