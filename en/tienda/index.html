Before choosing your piece

Where do you ship?

We ship to every European Union country for €14.99 per order, including Spain. We prepare your order within 24 hours. Transit time is additional; check the delivery timeframe before confirming your order.

Can I collect my order?

Yes, pickup is free at La Mesa, Carrer de l’Atlàntida, 47, Barcelona. Your order will be ready for pickup within 24 hours. We will notify you and confirm collection details.

Are the pieces in the photos for sale?

The images show our work. In the shop, you can check available pieces, their photos, prices and details before buying.
